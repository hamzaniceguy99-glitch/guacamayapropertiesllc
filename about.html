Why keeping changes everything

A buyer who intends to sell next quarter renovates for photographs. One who intends to keep renovates for the roof.

How we work

Buy direct, fix properly, lease fairly

Buying directly removes the commission, the listing period and the chain of buyers who may or may not close. For an owner with a timeline, certainty is often worth more than the headline number.

Renovating for the long term means licensed trades and permits where the work requires them. It costs more at the time and far less across the years we'll own the property.

And leasing has to be consistent. The same criteria for every applicant, a written lease that explains both sides' obligations, and repairs that actually get answered.
